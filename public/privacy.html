Privacy policy

Jot, at https://jot.deathstalker.dev. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Jot is a small personal notes app run by one person for friends and family. This page says what it keeps about you and what it does with it.

What Jot keeps

  • Your Google profile: your name, email address and profile picture. Jot gets them when you sign in with Google, and uses them to recognise you and to show your name to people you share a note with.
  • Your notes: the text you write. They are stored in your browser on your device, and a copy is stored on Cloudflare (the service that hosts Jot) so they sync between your devices.
  • Sharing details: which notes you share, with whom, and the invite links you make.

What Jot does not do

  • It does not show ads, and it does not use analytics or tracking.
  • It does not sell your data or give it to anyone else.
  • It sets one cookie, the sign-in session cookie that keeps you signed in. It sets a short lived cookie when you open an invite link. There are no other cookies.
  • It asks Google only for your basic profile (name, email, picture). Nothing else.

Who can see your notes

Only you, and anyone you share a note with. People you share a note with can read it, and edit it if you gave them edit access. You can remove them at any time. Text a person already synced to their own device stays on that device. The person who runs Jot can technically access stored data, as with any app you do not host yourself.

Where it is stored

Notes and account details are stored with Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, Durable Objects and D1). Sign-in is handled by Google. Both have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

To have your account and notes deleted, email 0xsoumya@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. Signing out in the app also removes the copy stored in that browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
